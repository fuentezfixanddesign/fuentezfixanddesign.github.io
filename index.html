Where the work happens

I'm not a mobile detailer — I don't carry a water tank. There are two ways to get your car done, and the listed prices are the drop-off prices.

At my driveway — listed price

Drop the car off at my driveway in New Windsor. I supply the water and power, and it's the best setup for the polish and correction tiers — shade, good light, and no time pressure.

  • All five tiers available
  • One car per appointment — your car gets the whole block
  • Best choice for Tier 4 and Tier 5 work

At your home — +$25 travel & setup

Within about 15 miles (farther by quote), I can come to you — but only if you have both of these, because I have no water tank to bring:

  • An outdoor hose I can use, and
  • An outdoor power outlet for the vacuum and buffers

I bring everything else. Without a hose, I can only do interior-only work — exterior tiers get switched to drop-off or rescheduled. Room to open all the doors, and shade if you have it, helps a lot. Dropping off at my driveway saves the $25 — that's the honest version of the discount.

Straight talk on wax: Spray wax is a quick gloss topper — about 2–4 weeks. Cleaner wax (Tier 3) cleans and protects in one buffer pass, which is right for a mildly dull daily driver. Carnauba (Tiers 4–5) is about the look — a deep, warm shine for about 4–8 weeks, longer if the car is garaged. I don't offer ceramic coatings or synthetic sealants, and I won't sell you an expensive wax as “months of protection” — what you see above is what each one actually does.
